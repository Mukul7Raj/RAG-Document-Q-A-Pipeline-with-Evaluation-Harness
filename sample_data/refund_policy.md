# Global Customer Refund and Hardware Warranty Policy
Document ID: POL-REF-2026-B
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

## 1. Standard Return Window
Customers may initiate a return for physical hardware products within 30 calendar days from the confirmed date of delivery. To be eligible for a full refund, items must be in their original packaging, unblemished, and accompanied by all original accessories, manuals, and documentation.

## 2. Restocking Fees
For opened electronics, workstations, and high-performance server components:
- A mandatory 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund subtotal if the box seal has been opened or broken.
- Unopened hardware returned in pristine, factory-sealed condition incurs zero restocking fee.
- Defective or damaged items received upon arrival are exempt from restocking fees, provided notice is submitted within 72 hours of carrier delivery.

## 3. Non-Returnable and Non-Refundable Items
The following product categories are strictly non-refundable and cannot be returned under any circumstances:
1. Downloadable digital software licenses and API subscription keys once revealed or activated.
2. Custom configured-to-order (CTO) enterprise servers and custom laser-engraved devices.
3. Intimate wearable sensors, open earplugs, or items classified as personal hygiene products once the sterile seal is breached.
4. Promotional gift cards, promotional vouchers, or bundle rebates.

## 4. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Workflow
Returns without a pre-approved Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number will be rejected at the warehouse dock:
- Step 1: The customer submits an RMA request via the online portal providing order ID, serial number, and reason for return.
- Step 2: The support team reviews and issues an RMA number within 2 business days.
- Step 3: The customer securely packages the unit and affixes the prepaid shipping label. The RMA number must be legibly written on the exterior shipping carton.
- Step 4: Upon warehouse receipt and hardware diagnostic inspection, refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## 5. International Returns Shipping
For international orders outside the domestic shipping zone:
- The customer is responsible for international return postage and customs clearance duties.
- Import taxes, customs brokerage fees, and initial express delivery fees are non-refundable.
